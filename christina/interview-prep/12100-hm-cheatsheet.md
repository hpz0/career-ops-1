# 12100 Collective — HM Interview Cheat Sheet
**Christina Pizzi · Hiring Manager round**

---

## 90-Second Pitch

> I scale paid search portfolios profitably — nine years in agency, currently at Vincodo directing sixteen accounts and about a million a month in spend, plus a team of specialists.
>
> My best work is a national home services client — B2B lead gen. Over two years: **164%** more spend, **100%** more conversions, CPA up only **34%**. I got there by fixing tracking first, then PMAX and ROAS testing with guardrails, and building a team that could run without me in every account.
>
> Before that, AKQA — Fortune 500 CPG, million-dollar quarterly budgets, CPA targets, client pitches.
>
> Your posting is what I do today: own a client portfolio, run QBRs, mentor juniors, partner across channels. I'm at a point where I want a growing agency — more scope, more upside — not a smaller job. That's why I'm here.

*Practice out loud. Target 75–90 seconds. Lead with outcomes, not title.*

## Growth Frameworks (only gap on JD)

Pick **one framework + flagship client**. Don't recite the list.

| Client | Use | KPIs |
|--------|-----|------|
| B2B lead gen (home services) | Unit economics + ICE | CPL, lead quality, CPA |
| eCommerce (AKQA CPG) | LTV:CAC + ROAS | Revenue, ROAS, AOV |
| Low data / new tests | ICE | Stack-rank, don't spray tests |
| High spend / scaling | Incrementality | Holdouts before budget jumps |

**If asked directly:**
> "I match framework to business model. Lead gen — CPL and what a qualified lead is worth; eCommerce — ROAS and LTV:CAC. I prioritize tests with ICE — on my flagship, a CallRail tracking fix beat any bid change on impact. Before scaling spend I ask if conversions are incremental or demand we'd capture anyway."

**One-liners if they name a term:**
- **LTV:CAC** — paying less to acquire than they're worth; below ~3:1, fix conversion before scaling
- **Unit economics** — profit per lead/order after media; home services CPA had to fit booked-job value
- **Incrementality** — would this convert without the ad? holdouts/geo tests before big budget adds
- **AARRR** — acquisition is my lane; I flag when CPL is fine but close rate drops (wrong funnel stage)
- **ICE** — Impact × Confidence × Ease; LP fix before low-budget audience tests

## Expected Q&A

**Tell me about yourself** → use pitch above.

**Why this role / why leave Vincodo?**
> "Vincodo has been great — three promotions in nine years. I'm ready for the next chapter: a growing agency where I can take on broader growth scope, help build the team, and have real upside through equity. I'm not leaving because something's broken; I'm leaving because I've outgrown the lane I'm in and want somewhere that's scaling."

**Why 12100?**
> "You're at the stage where senior people still run accounts and shape how the agency grows — that's what I want. The client mix fits my background, the role has cross-channel scope, and the equity piece matters at this point in my career."

**Director → Senior on the posting?**
> "I'm a Director today doing this scope — portfolio, clients, team. I'd want the title to match, whether that's Director now or a clear path within six months. Is that possible?"

**Why should we hire you?**
> "Most candidates either run campaigns or manage people. I do both — **$1M/mo**, **16 accounts**, team that stays, and a flagship scale story with numbers. You need someone client-facing who can also grow juniors. That's my current job."

**Biggest weakness / what are you still building?**
> "Growth framework vocabulary and formal full-funnel ownership outside search — I've lived unit economics and test prioritization on accounts, but I'm sharper on search than email. I'm here because this role closes that gap; I learn fast and I partner well with channel specialists."

**eCommerce and B2B — can you do both?**
> "Yes. Flagship is B2B lead gen at scale — CPL, lead quality, offline conv, call tracking. AKQA was Fortune 500 CPG — ROAS, CPA, quarterly budgets up to $1M. Different KPIs, same discipline: match spend to what a conversion is worth."

**Tell me about scaling an account.**
> Plateau → rebuilt structure, tracking fix, PMAX/ROAS tests → **164% / 100% / 34%** over 2 yrs.

**Account underperforming — what do you do?**
> "Diagnose before optimizing. Tracking clean? Right KPI for the business model? Structure or budget pacing issue? On my flagship we couldn't scale until CallRail matched platform data — fix that first, then tests. I stack-rank with ICE so we're not tweaking bids when the LP is broken."

**How do you prioritize across a portfolio?**
> "Revenue and risk. Flagship and highest-spend accounts get daily; long tail gets weekly cadence. Protect efficiency on the biggest before scaling the rest. Forecasting and budget allocation are part of my director role today."

**Mentoring juniors?**
> Prioritization, QA on campaigns, performance reviews → **reduced turnover**, specialists doing strategy not just execution. Same model as your 1–2 direct reports.

**Tracking / measurement?**
> CTM/CallRail audit + tech partners → fixed attribution → unlocked scaling. GTM/GA4/server-side — partner with conversion/CRO lead; not my deepest lane yet, but I know when data is wrong.

**Paid social — search-heavy?**
> "Core is search. Ran Meta, Pinterest, display early career. Now I QA social plans and partner with specialists — catch bad targeting or attribution misalignment. Enough to lead strategy, not claim I'm the daily Meta buyer."

**Email?**
> "Not my daily channel. I'd partner with your email specialist, own cross-channel alignment, learn your stack in 30 days. Won't oversell."

**Client pushback / disagree with client on strategy?**
> "Data first. Show the numbers, propose a test with guardrails, let results decide. I've presented to senior marketing leaders at AKQA and run QBRs on high-value accounts — firm on recommendations, collaborative on how we test."

**First 90 days?**
> **30:** Audit accounts — tracking, structure, KPI fit. Meet social, analytics, CRO. **60:** Quick wins + one ICE-prioritized test; QBR rhythm. **90:** Roadmaps per account; junior mentorship cadence; one new growth opp each.

**Comp?**
> "**$125–130K** base given director scope; top of your band. Plus equity structure."

**Remote from PA?**
> "Remote works — EST, ~90 min from Brooklyn. In for QBRs/team days. Not relocating."

## Questions to Ask Back

1. How is the portfolio split between eCommerce and B2B lead gen today, and which type of accounts would I own first if I joined?
2. When a client needs cross-channel strategy, who owns the call on search-led versus social-led — me, the Paid Social Director, or does it vary by account?
3. What does success look like for this role at six months — are you measuring client retention, account KPIs, team development, or something else?
4. Is this a new headcount or a backfill, and what does the path to Director or Head of Paid Media look like from this seat?
5. The posting mentions equity — how is that structured for senior hires, and what does the vesting schedule look like?
6. 12100 is a newer agency, and I'm excited about that pace, but I'd like to understand stability — what's client retention been like, and is revenue growth coming mostly from new logos or expansion on existing accounts?
7. Who would I report to day to day, and how does this role work with the Paid Social and analytics leads on shared accounts?
8. What surprised you most about building the paid media team here — good or bad — that someone joining should know upfront?
