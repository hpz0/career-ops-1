## Story 1: Flagship Client Scale-Up

**Situation:** Vincodo's largest client — national home services — had plateaued on paid search volume. Leadership wanted aggressive growth without destroying unit economics.

**Task:** Scale media spend significantly while maintaining or improving CPA/ROAS efficiency.

**Action:** Christina (then Senior Manager) rebuilt account structure, expanded keyword/campaign coverage, introduced ROAS bidding and PMAX testing, and worked with the client on landing page and tracking improvements. Ran structured budget allocation across the 16-account portfolio to protect efficiency on the flagship.

**Result:** **164% media spend increase, 100% conversion lift, only 34% CPA increase over two years** — the foundation that led to her Director promotion.

**Best for:** "Tell me about a time you scaled performance" · "How do you balance growth vs. efficiency?" · hiring-manager scope questions
