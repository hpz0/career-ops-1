## Role Description
As the Strategic Procurement Manager, you will be part of a dynamic Supply Chain Management (SCM) Team. Working together with the SCM and market teams, you will be on point to project manage and execute strategic sourcing initiatives supporting the successful deployment and continuous improvement of supply chain operations. This role will oversee supplier relationships, negotiate contracts, manage SLAs and drive cost-optimization to achieve current operational and future growth goals.

## In this role, you'll:
- Strategic Sourcing: Lead end-to-end strategic sourcing, supplier onboarding and product prioritization.
- Procurement Strategy: Establish a procurement playbook to ensure critical hardware, and materials are prioritized, sourced, and scheduled effectively.
- Vendor Management: Develop a supplier dashboard that provides methods to track vendor SLA's, correct exceptions, and adapt procurement strategies as needed.
- Process Optimization: Lead Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) development to drive consistent, quality purchasing and contracting processes.
- Risk Mitigation: Proactively identify and assess supply chain vulnerabilities by developing multi-sourcing strategies, monitoring vendor financial health, and creating long-term contingency plans to ensure supply continuity.
