# Senior Procurement Manager — Ingredients, Packaging & Strategic Sourcing — Levels

About the Role

 The Senior Procurement Manager is responsible for developing and executing the sourcing strategy for Levels' rapidly growing ingredient, packaging, and contract manufacturing spend across both our Protein Powder and Functional Beverage businesses. Reporting directly to the Chief Supply Chain Officer, this role leads supplier negotiations, strategic sourcing initiatives, commodity purchasing strategy, cost optimization, supplier relationship management, and procurement risk management across a rapidly expanding global supply base. This is not simply a purchasing role. It is a strategic procurement leadership position responsible for helping Levels build the supplier network capable of supporting one of the fastest-growing premium nutrition brands in North America.

 Responsibilities

 Strategic Procurement

 - Develop sourcing strategies across ingredients, packaging, and contract manufacturing.
- Lead supplier selection, qualification, and ongoing supplier management.
- Identify opportunities to improve cost, quality, service, and supply resiliency.
- Build long-term supplier partnerships that support future company growth.

 Supplier & Contract Management

 - Lead commercial negotiations for ingredient, packaging, and manufacturing agreements.
- Negotiate pricing, payment terms, lead times, service levels, rebates, and long-term supply agreements.
- Conduct supplier business reviews and performance evaluations.
- Develop alternate suppliers to reduce business risk and improve supply continuity.

 Commodity & Cost Management

 - Monitor commodity markets and purchasing trends across key raw materials.
- Develop buying strategies based on market conditions, forecasts, inventory positions, and demand.
- Partner with Finance on commodity forecasting and procurement planning.
- Lead annual cost savings and value engineering initiatives while maintaining product quality.
- Evaluate ingredient substitutions, packaging optimization, and supplier productivity opportunities.

 New Product Commercialization

 - Partner closely with Product Development, Product Commercialization, Supply Chain, and Operations to support new product launches.
- Responsibilities include:
- Sourcing new ingredients and packaging
- Supplier qualification
- Commercial readiness
- Capacity planning
- Cost modeling
- Launch support

 Procurement Risk Management

 - Develop strategies to proactively manage procurement risk.
- Monitor and mitigate risks related to:
- Commodity volatility
- Tariffs
- Capacity constraints
- Sole-source suppliers
- Supplier financial health
- Business continuity
- Regulatory requirements
- Geopolitical events

 Cross-Functional Leadership

 - Partner closely with:
- Supply Chain
- Product Development
- Product Commercialization
- Quality
- Regulatory
- Finance
- Operations
- Manufacturing partners
- Support executive planning through supplier intelligence, procurement reporting, market trends, and sourcing recommendations.

 Procurement Operations

 - Help build a world-class procurement function.
- Improve procurement processes and sourcing workflows.
- Build supplier scorecards and procurement KPIs.
- Develop reporting dashboards and market updates.
- Improve inventory efficiency and working capital.
- Support procurement planning for continued rapid growth.

 What Success Looks Like

 - Strategic supplier partnerships continue to strengthen.
- Ingredient and packaging costs remain competitive despite commodity volatility.
- Supply disruptions are minimized through proactive sourcing strategies.
- Procurement supports rapid commercialization without delaying launches.
