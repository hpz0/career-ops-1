## Nuclear 101 (30-second version — you knew nothing yesterday)

**Baseload** = power that’s on almost all the time (nuclear’s pitch vs intermittent renewables).

**PWR (Pressurized Water Reactor)** — most common US type; their guide says learn this:  
Fuel heats water in a **closed pressurized loop** → heat to a second loop → **steam → turbine → electricity**.

**Fleet-scale** = build the **same** plant playbook many times so supply chain and construction get cheaper/faster each copy.

**If asked “do you know nuclear?”**  
> I don’t come from a utility nuclear background — and this role is Indirect/Corporate, not safety-class materials. I’ve done the homework on fleet-scale, design-once-build-many, and PWRs. Day one I bring category strategy for IT, professional services, and workplace spend. I’ll learn the domain vocabulary fast; I won’t fake reactor physics.
