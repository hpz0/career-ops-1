## Likely Questions — VP Finance pack

All questions `[inferred from JD]` / first-procurement-hire under Finance archetype unless noted. Answers use only `cv.md` / story-bank facts.

### 1. Walk me through how you’d stand up procurement here from scratch.

**Headline:** Diagnose → govern → control the flow → then scale headcount.

**Effect:** Finance gets spend visibility and compliance without a multi-year science project.

**Rationale / ops (Plenty-shaped):**
1. Map who buys what across sites/entities (parts, facilities, IT, contract labor, project/systems vendors).
2. Publish policy + approval matrix + vendor onboarding with Finance/Legal.
3. Enforce PR→PO→receipt→invoice in the current ERP; fix maverick paths.
4. Attack top categories for savings/continuity.
5. Hire only when volume outruns control quality — player-coach first (Plenty: managed 3 buyers after foundation, not before).

### 2. Tell me about a time you built or transformed a procurement / P2P function.

**Story:** S1 — Coupa P2P + policy + team of 3 (Plenty).

**Answer shape:**
- **Situation:** Fragmented / manual buying; needed company-wide discipline.
- **Task:** Lead Coupa P2P + operating policy with a small team.
- **Action:** Implementation + published/enforced Procurement Policy; partnered Finance, Legal, IT; managed 3 buyers.
- **Result:** **99% spend on contract**; PR cycle **3 → 1.5 days**.
- **Reflection:** Platform without policy and behavior change fails — own both.

### 3. What does “good” look like for Finance in year one?

**Scoreboard offer:**
- % spend on contract / under policy
- PR and PO cycle time
- Supplier e-invoice / exception rate (AP labor proxy)
- Documented savings vs budget on priority categories
- Cleaner vendor master + fewer audit/AP exceptions

**Plenty anchors:** 99% on contract; 3→1.5 days; e-invoice +50%.

### 4. How do you partner with AP / Controllership?

**Story:** S2 — electronic invoicing.

**Point:** Best AP relief is often upstream — vendor enablement and PO quality — not only AP headcount. At Plenty, driving supplier e-invoice adoption **+50%** reduced AP labor. I’d own vendor master hygiene, PO accuracy, and invoice channels; AP owns payment run / close; we share exception dashboards.

### 5. What’s your first 90 days?

| Window | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Days 1–30 | Stakeholder map (ops, parts, systems/projects, IT, AP); spend & process baseline; list top control gaps |
| Days 31–60 | Draft policy + approval matrix; quick wins on top maverick categories; vendor onboarding standard |
| Days 61–90 | Embed PR/PO discipline; e-invoice / invoice quality push with AP; present 12-month roadmap (people / process / systems) to you |

### 6. How do you handle maverick spend and pushy stakeholders?

**Headline:** Make compliant the easy path; escalate on risk and thresholds.

**Ops:** Clear thresholds and catalogs; fast turnaround on legitimate needs (cycle-time credibility); educate on total cost and contract risk; escalate only when policy or audit exposure is real — not ego contests.

### 7. When do you hire the next buyer vs stay lean?

**Headline:** Headcount follows proven volume and control gaps — not org charts.

**Plenty:** Ran a team of 3 with published policy once the operating model existed. At Pengate I’d stay player-coach until transaction volume, multi-site coordination, or category depth justifies a specialist hire — and I’d bring you a business case (volume, cycle time, risk), not a wish list.

### 8. Talk about systems — ERP, P2P, expense.

**Proof:** Led Coupa (P2P), Ironclad + DocuSign SpringCM (CLM), TripActions (travel), NetSuite ERP/MRP migration (S1, S3, S4).

**Framing for Pengate:** Assess what’s live (ERP PO module, Concur adjacency from Finance hiring). Optimize controls in current tools first; sequence any P2P/CLM investment after requirements from Finance — don’t lead with “buy Coupa.”

### 9. Give me a savings / negotiation example.

**Story:** S5 — **30% BOM cost reduction in six months** via value engineering + supplier negotiations.

**Bridge to dealer world:** Same commercial muscle applies to parts, facilities, contract labor, and project vendors — TCO and supplier performance, not only unit price. For this VP, pair with compliance metrics so you don’t read as “sourcing only.”

### 10. Why this role / why leave Comcast after promotion?

**Headline:** Builder ownership under Finance beats category-only enterprise buying for what I want next.

**Script:**
> The Senior Manager promotion at Comcast confirmed I can lead at Fortune-50 scale. What I’m optimizing for now is standing up and owning the procurement operating model end-to-end — policy, process, systems, and partnership with Finance — like I did at Plenty. Pengate’s growth and the need to build that function from scratch is the mandate I’m looking for.

- Spend under management / on-contract %

### What NOT to do
- Lead with agtech BOM / FDA as the hero (translate or demote).

3. What’s the 12-month definition of success for this seat?
4. Current ERP / PO / AP / Concur landscape — what’s sacred vs open?
5. Where should early political capital go — parts, facilities, IT, or systems/project vendors?
